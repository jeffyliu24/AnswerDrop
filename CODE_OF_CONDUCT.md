# Code of conduct

We want a welcoming, useful technical community. Be respectful, assume good intent, give specific feedback, and make room for different experience levels. Harassment, threats, discrimination, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation to protect contributors. If a conduct issue occurs, contact a maintainer privately through the repository's contact options. Do not include sensitive personal details in a public issue. We will review reports promptly and keep details limited to people who need them.
